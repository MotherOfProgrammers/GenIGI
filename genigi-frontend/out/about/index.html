Old missions.
New company.

GenIGI is a player-first home for people who still care about Project IGI—and want to see it treated with the respect a classic deserves.

We remember the feeling.

The quiet before a patrol turns. The satisfaction of planning a route and getting through on your own terms. Project IGI made space for a kind of grounded, deliberate action that stuck with us.

GenIGI brings longtime fans and new players together around the same idea: improve the experience where it needs care, and protect the original story and feel wherever it doesn’t.

This is a community in progress. We’ll share what’s real, be open about what’s early, and make room for people who want to help.

“Make the way back feel welcoming. Keep the mission itself intact.”

GenIGI community principle / 001

A steady hand.

Not every old game needs to become something else. We believe the best improvements know when to stop.

Keep the story

Campaign, characters, and tone are part of the point. They’re not obstacles to modernization.

Be honest

Work in progress means work in progress. No imaginary release buttons, downloads, or timelines.

Welcome in

Whether IGI was your first tactical game or you just discovered it, you belong in the conversation.
